Detailed Description

The linalg::blas_backend on an opencl_context: gemm and gemv on the device, the rest on the host.

Trivially copyable (one pointer), as the concept requires; pass it by value to core's free functions. float runs on the device; double too when the device has cl_khr_fp64; any other scalar type, on the host.
