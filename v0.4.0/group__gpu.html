How the products of float are computed.

Enumerator
float32 

In float throughout (cuBLAS's default): the reference's rounding, to the order of the sums.

tf32 

Tensor cores, the inputs rounded to TF32 (10 bits of mantissa), the sums in float: several times faster, each product within about 1e-3 of the float result, relative to its scale.
