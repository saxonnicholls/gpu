Detailed Description

A weight matrix in one of core's quantised formats, kept in an OpenCL device's memory: made by opencl_quant_kernels::upload, read by the products of opencl_quant_backend.

A handle: copies share the device memory, which is released with the last of them. rows and cols are members, as on core's views, so ml::lora_weights takes it as its frozen base.
